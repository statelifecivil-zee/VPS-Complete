Product Summary

What is State Life Islamic Retirement Savings Fund (SLIRSF)?

State Life Islamic Retirement Savings Fund (SLIRSF) is an open-ended Shariah-compliant Voluntary Pension Scheme established under the Voluntary Pension System Rules 2005. It provides Pakistani citizens and non-resident Pakistanis a secure, disciplined, and tax-efficient mechanism to build a dedicated retirement chest.

Investment Objective

To provide participants with a secure source of retirement savings and regular halal income post-retirement, tailored to their individual risk appetites through Shariah-compliant investments.

Shariah Governance

Operated on the Islamic principle of Wakalah tul Istismar (Investment Agency Agreement). Supervised by reputable Shariah Advisors ensuring complete adherence to Islamic jurisprudence.

Built-In Takaful Coverage

Participants enjoy built-in Tayyab Takaful protection offering financial coverage for total permanent disability or unexpected death, safeguarding family members against life's uncertainties.

CORE ADVANTAGES

Key Benefits of Investing in SLIRSF

Up to 20% Tax Credit

Claim an immediate tax rebate on annual contributions up to 20% of your annual taxable income under Section 63 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001.

50% Tax-Free Lump Sum

At retirement (between ages 60–70 or 25 years from first contribution), withdraw up to 50% of your accumulated fund balance completely tax-free.

Dynamic Allocation Schemes

Select between High, Medium, Low, Lower Volatility, Life Cycle, or 100% Customized Allocation schemes across three specialized sub-funds.

Tax-Free PF & VPS Transfer

Seamlessly roll over balances from recognized Provident Funds, Gratuity Funds, or existing Pension Fund Managers (PFMs) into SLIRSF without any tax penalty.

Best-in-Class Life Annuities

Convert the remaining balance into State Life's market-leading Life Annuities for guaranteed post-retirement income.

Low Minimum Contribution

Begin with an initial contribution of only PKR 1,000 and subsequent contributions of just PKR 500 at any frequency—monthly, quarterly, or annually.

Takaful Protection

Complimentary Coverage Benefits

State Life provides valuable complimentary coverage to its VPS members, ensuring your financial legacy remains secure against life's uncertainties.

Basic Death Benefit

In case of a VPS member's death due to any cause, State Life will pay an amount equal to the member's VPS balance, up to a maximum of Rs 12 million.

Accidental Death Benefit

If a member dies within 90 days due to accidental injuries, additional compensation equal to the VPS balance (maximum Rs 12 million) will be paid.

Permanent Total Disability Benefit (Accident)

If a member becomes permanently disabled within 90 days due to accidental injuries, State Life will pay an amount equal to the VPS balance, up to Rs 12 million.

Permanent Total Disability Benefit (Sickness)

In case of permanent and total disability due to illness or natural causes, the member will receive an amount equal to the VPS balance, up to Rs 12 million.

Hospitalization Benefit (For Investment Amount Rs. 2 million & above)

Covers inpatient care, daycare surgeries, and pre/post-hospitalization costs through State Life's network hospitals. Coverage equals 10% of the VPS balance, capped at Rs 1 million.

Fund Architecture

Three Specialized Sub-Funds

Each contribution is invested across three dedicated sub-funds based on selected asset allocation schemes.

High Growth

Equity Sub-Fund

Invests primarily in KMI-30 Shariah-compliant equities to maximize long-term capital appreciation.

Moderate

Debt Sub-Fund

Invests in Shariah-compliant Sukuks, Islamic bank deposits, and rated debt instruments for steady income.

Low Risk

Money Market Sub-Fund

Focuses on short-term Islamic money market instruments offering high liquidity and principal safety.
