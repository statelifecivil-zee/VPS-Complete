Product Overview

What is State Life Retirement Savings Fund?

State Life Retirement Savings Fund (SLRSF) is an open-ended Voluntary Pension Scheme established under SECP's Voluntary Pension System Rules, 2005. It provides Pakistani citizens and overseas Pakistanis a structured, professionally managed mechanism to build a disciplined retirement corpus with massive income tax advantages.

Fund Objective

To provide participants with a reliable source of post-retirement income and long-term capital accumulation by investing in a diversified portfolio of traditional equity, debt, and money market instruments.

Institutional Stewardship

Managed directly by State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan (AM1 rated) with Digital Custodian Company Limited (DCCL) acting as the independent Trustee to ensure strict regulatory safety.

Complimentary Coverage

All enrolled participants are automatically entitled to valuable complimentary death, accidental disability, and hospitalization benefits backed by State Life up to Rs 12 Million.

Core Advantages

Key Benefits of Investing in SLRSF

Up to 20% Tax Credit

Claim an immediate tax rebate on annual contributions up to 20% of your annual taxable income under Section 63 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001.

50% Tax-Free Lump Sum

At retirement (between ages 60-70 or 25 years from first contribution), withdraw up to 50% of your accumulated fund balance completely tax-free.

Dynamic Allocation Schemes

Select between High, Medium, Low, Lower Volatility, Life Cycle, or 100% Customized Allocation schemes across three specialized sub-funds.

Tax-Free PF & VPS Transfer

Seamlessly roll over balances from recognized Provident Funds, Gratuity Funds, or existing Pension Fund Managers (PFMs) into SLRSF without any tax penalty.

Best-in-Class Life Annuities

Convert the remaining balance into State Life's market-leading Life Annuities for guaranteed post-retirement income.

Low Minimum Contribution

Begin with an initial contribution of only PKR 1,000 and subsequent contributions of just PKR 500 at any frequency—monthly, quarterly, or annually.

Automatic Financial Security

Complimentary Coverage Benefits

As an SLRSF participant, you and your family are entitled to complimentary life and health protections funded by State Life, matching your VPS balance up to Rs 12 Million.

Basic Death Benefit

In case of a VPS member's death due to any cause, State Life will pay an amount equal to the member's VPS balance, up to a maximum of Rs 12 million.

Accidental Death Benefit

If a member dies within 90 days due to accidental injuries, an additional compensation equal to the VPS balance (maximum Rs 12 million) will be paid.

Permanent Total Disability Benefit (Accident)

If a member becomes permanently disabled within 90 days due to accidental injuries, State Life will pay an amount equal to the VPS balance, up to Rs 12 million.

Permanent Total Disability Benefit (Sickness)

In case of permanent and total disability due to illness or natural causes, the member will receive an amount equal to the VPS balance, up to Rs 12 million.

Hospitalization Benefit (For Investment Rs. 2 Million & Above)

Covers inpatient medical care, daycare surgeries, and pre/post-hospitalization costs through State Life's nationwide network hospitals. Coverage equals 10% of VPS balance, capped at Rs 1 million.

Fund Architecture

Three Dedicated SLRSF Sub-Funds

Under the Offering Document, SLRSF consists of three sub-funds, each suited to a different level of risk.

High Growth

SLRSF - Equity Sub-Fund

Invests in listed shares on the Pakistan Stock Exchange for long-term growth.

Moderate Risk

SLRSF - Debt Sub-Fund

Invests in government securities, bank deposits and rated debt for steady returns.

Low Risk

SLRSF - Money Market Sub-Fund

Invests in short-term treasury bills and cash to keep your money safe and easy to access.
